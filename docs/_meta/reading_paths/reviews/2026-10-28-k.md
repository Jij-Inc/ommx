OMMX owns the common meaning of the problem and candidate evaluation. The
modeler owns the source business model; an Adapter owns backend input/output
conversion and evaluates decoded candidates against its input Instance; the
backend owns its search and execution reports. Preparation membership, value
reconstruction, feasibility, objective values, and optimality are separate
facts. Artifact and Experiment are optional storage/exchange and trial-recording
layers. The entry pages preserve those boundaries.
